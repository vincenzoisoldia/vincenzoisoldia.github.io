I am a Ph.D. student at the [Max Planck Institute for Mathematics in the Sciences](https://www.mis.mpg.de) in Leipzig, in the *Geometry, Groups, and Dynamics* group, supervised by Prof. Dr. [Anna Wienhard](https://www.mis.mpg.de/people/anna-wienhard).

My interests have always been quite broad, but they are held together by Geometry. I find topology and dynamics especially exciting, and I am fascinated by the interplay of analytic, differential, and algebraic techniques in answering geometric questions. Having just started my Ph.D., I am still shaping my identity as a mathematician, and I am currently learning about Anosov representations and (higher) Teichmüller theory. I am always happy to discuss these or related topics, so feel free to reach out through any of the links above.

Before starting my Ph.D., I was a pre-doctoral fellow at the [Max Planck Institute for Mathematics in the Sciences](https://www.mis.mpg.de) and at the [Max Planck Institute of Cell Biology and Genetics](https://www.mpi-cbg.de), working with Prof. [Türkü Özlüm Çelik](https://sites.google.com/view/tocj/home) and Prof. [İrem Portakal](https://www.irem-portakal.de) on the dynamics of interacting species. This is another topic I am passionate about, and I am always happy to discuss and/or collaborate on it!

I received my M.Sc. in Mathematics from the [University of Trento](https://www.unitn.it/en), with a thesis on Schur apolarity and secant varieties of flag varieties, supervised by Prof. [Alessandra Bernardi](https://sites.google.com/unitn.it/alessandra-bernardi/home) and Dr. [Stefano Canino](https://sites.google.com/view/stefanocanino/home-page). I obtained my B.Sc. from the [University of Milan](https://www.unimi.it/en), with a final seminar on plane algebraic curves and Plücker formulas.
